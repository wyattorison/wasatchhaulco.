How it works

From photos to swept floor

  1. Send photos

    Before Thursday evening, by the form below or by text. No visit needed for most jobs.

  2. Get a firm price

    Priced by how much of the trailer you fill. The quote includes landfill fees.

  3. We haul Saturday

    Two people, straps and dollies, in the time window you picked. You don't lift anything.

  4. Donate, recycle, dump

    Good furniture goes to local donation centers and metal gets recycled before anything hits the landfill.

What we can't take

  • Paint, solvents, chemicals
  • Gasoline, oil, propane tanks
  • Asbestos
  • Medical waste

Salt Lake County runs a household hazardous waste drop-off for these. Ask us and we'll point you to it.
